Help

Put the Studio on your phone like an app. One time, under a minute.

On an iPhone
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom (the square with the arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
On an Android phone
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.
On a computer
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the small install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. It opens in its own window, right from your dock or taskbar.
Text too small?

Tap the AA button at the top of any page to make everything larger. It remembers.
